01Overview

NozzleCast is a monitoring and control client for Bambuddy, a self-hosted 3D-printer management server. It connects directly to the Bambuddy instance and, optionally, the push-notification relay that you set up and run yourself — typically on your own home network or personal server.

There is no NozzleCast cloud service in between. The people who make NozzleCast never receive, store, or have access to your printer data, your server address, your API keys, or your usage of the app. This policy exists to be precise about that, not to disclose a hidden data pipeline.

02What you configure

Setting up NozzleCast means entering connection details for infrastructure you already control:

Bambuddy server

Your server's URL and API key, entered in Settings. Stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only. Sent only to the host you typed in, over the connection you configured.

Push relay (optional)

If you run nozzlecast-relay for Live Activities, its URL and auth secret are stored the same way — on-device, sent only to your relay.

Firebase config (optional)

A GoogleService-Info.plist you import from your own Firebase project, used solely to subscribe to the ntfy alert topic your Bambuddy instance already publishes to.

03Printer & filament data

While you use the app, NozzleCast reads live data from your Bambuddy server — printer status, job progress, temperatures, AMS filament slots, camera snapshots, and your filament inventory — and displays it. This data:

● stays between you and your server

It is fetched over the connection you configured and rendered on your device. It is not copied to any third-party service.

● is cached only briefly, locally

NozzleCast keeps an in-memory copy so the app feels responsive; it does not maintain its own database of your print history.

04Camera & scanning

The Scan tab uses your device's camera to read filament spool barcodes and printed labels, via Apple's on-device VisionKit text and barcode recognition. Frames are processed entirely on your device and are never recorded, uploaded, or sent anywhere — not to Bambuddy, not to a NozzleCast server (which doesn't exist), not to Apple beyond the standard on-device Vision framework.

05Push notifications & Live Activities

To show a Live Activity while your phone is locked, iOS issues push tokens that NozzleCast registers with your own relay server so it can send updates directly to your device via Apple's push service:

  • Your device's APNs token and each Live Activity's push token are sent only to the relay URL you configured — infrastructure you run.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and, for ntfy-based alerts, Firebase Cloud Messaging handle final delivery to your device, as they do for any iOS app that sends notifications.
  • None of these tokens are visible to, or usable by, anyone other than your own relay and Apple's push infrastructure.

06What we don't do

noAnalytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind
noAdvertising or ad identifiers
noNozzleCast-operated accounts, servers, or databases
noSharing or selling of any data, to anyone, ever
noAccess to your Bambuddy data by the app's developer

07Retention & deletion

Your server URL, API key, and relay credentials live in the iOS Keychain for as long as NozzleCast is installed. Deleting the app removes them. Printer and filament data is never persisted outside your own Bambuddy server — there is nothing else to delete on our end, because nothing else exists.

08Children's privacy

NozzleCast is a technical tool for operating 3D-printer infrastructure and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, beyond what's described above — all of which is configuration you provide to connect to your own equipment.
